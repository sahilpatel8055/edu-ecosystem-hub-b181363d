## What is an online MBA in International Business?

An Online MBA in International Business is a 2-year degree course designed for students like you looking to enhance their business skills without taking a break from careers. You will experience interactive virtual sessions and live faculty interactions. You will study subjects such as Accounting, Finance, Marketing and Human Resources. This course teaches you about international trade intricacies, including import/export regulations, customs procedures, and international trade finance.

## FAQs

### Q1. What is an Online MBA in International Business?

An Online MBA in International Business is a 2-year degree for students enhancing business skills while working. You will have virtual sessions and live interactions, covering subjects like Accounting, Finance, Marketing, Human Resources, and international trade regulations.

### Q2. Is an Online MBA in International Business worth it?

Yes, If you want to advance your business skills and career opportunities in the global marketplace without taking a career break.

### Q3. What jobs can you get with an Online MBA in International Business?

You can get the jobs in various sector such as International Marketing Manager, Global Supply Chain Manager, Trade Compliance Specialist and international Financial Analyst.

### Q4. How long does an Online MBA in International Business take?

An Online MBA in International Business typically takes 2 years to complete.

### Q5. How much does an Online MBA in International Business cost?

The cost of an Online MBA in International Business can vary widely, typically ranging from INR 40,000 to INR 33 lakh depending on the institution.

### Q6. What are the career benefits of an Online MBA in International Business?

The career benefits of an Online MBA in International Business include enhanced global business skills, increased job opportunities, higher earning potential, and the ability to work in different roles such as International Marketing, Global Supply Chain Management, and International Finance.

### Q7. What skills will I gain from an Online MBA in International Business?

You will gain skills in Global marketing, international finance, cross-cultural communication and understanding international trade regulations.

### Q8. Is the Online MBA in International Business degree recognised globally?

Yes, an Online MBA in International Business is generally recognised globally as it is approved by UGC-DEB..

### Q9. What is the admission process for the Online MBA in International Business programme?

You can apply online through the institution’s website or visit their campus for admission details. Some programmes may require entrance exams like the GMAT or GRE, while others do not.

### Q10. How are exams conducted for the Online MBA in International Business programme?

Some universities conduct exams online, while others conduct them on campus.
