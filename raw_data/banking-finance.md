## Online MBA in Banking and Finance Overview 2024

[MBA in Banking and Finance](https://www.learningroutes.in/blog/mba-in-banking-and-finance) is a [postgraduate programme](https://www.learningroutes.in/blog/top-10-online-distance-pg-courses-in-india) that provides strong foundation in concepts like money management, fraud management, loan management, money laundering, and more. It enables students to integrate management practices and theories with principles of marketing, accounting, finance, and economics to solve business problems.

An Online MBA in Banking and Finance is divided into four semesters and covers a variety of specialised subjects. It additionally involves project work or dissertation that students are required to submit in their fourth semester.

This [online MBA programme](https://www.learningroutes.in/blog/online-mba-courses) is delivered in virtual mode via digital methods Online MBA in Banking and Finance classes can be taken with live audio-video lectures. Students can also access to pre-recorded lectures through the Learning Management System (LMS). While pursuing online MBA in Banking and Finance, it opens up a variety of career opportunities in the baking and financial sectors including bank manager, portfolio manager, budget analyst, and more.

Yes, MBA Banking and Finance Management Online Courses are approved by UGC/DEB/AICTE and also by the government of India. Students’ certificates are valid in all government and private organisations also. In fact, their Certificates can be verified by government organisations.

### Q2. Is this degree or certificate valid for abroad Study or Jobs?

Yes, Online MBA Banking and Finance Management is approved for study in India and abroad also.

### Q3. Can students pay a fee in instalments?

Yes, Students can pay their fees in instalments either semester-wise or year wise.

### Q4. What will be the examination mode?

The examination can be held in either offline mode or online mode at the concerned centres as per university rules.

### Q5. Do companies accept online MBAs?

The most important factor in determining the credibility and quality of online MBA programmes is accreditation. Accredited programmes ensure that the curriculum meets the necessary educational standards, so employers are more likely to hire candidates from accredited online MBA programmes.
