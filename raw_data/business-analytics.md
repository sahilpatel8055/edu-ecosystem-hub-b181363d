## Scrap the Dust From Online MBA in Business Analytics

An MBA in Business Analytics is a traditional management degree with a concentration in business analytics in online mode. This Master of Business Administration degree is of two years duration designed for candidates looking for niche roles and career advancement. The programme will train you in technical and [management skills](https://www.learningroutes.in/blog/top-skills-learnt-in-mba). The curriculum will prepare you to methodically understand how an organisation’s dataset is useful and how it can be used to increase productivity. The emphasis for this will be on statistical analysis to drive appropriate decision-making.

On completing this degree, you can pitch for jobs like Business Analyst, Data Analyst, Data Engineer, Predictive Modeler, Financial Analyst, etc. In addition, the online mode gives you an edge to continue to expand your work experience. Moreover, the online MBA is much more affordable than regular degrees. So the return on investment will also be high.

## Eligibility Criteria for Online MBA in Business Analytics

The eligibility criteria for different colleges may be different; however, the basic criteria are explained below:

- Must have completed a bachelor’s degree from a [recognised university](https://www.learningroutes.in/colleges).

- Candidates from any stream can apply for this programme, however, some institutions may ask for statistics and mathematics scores in the previous qualification.

- Students who have appeared in the final exam of their undergraduate and are awaiting their results can also apply.

- Work experience is not mandatory, but can be preferred in some colleges.

- Students with work experience and entrance exam scores get an exemption from online test/interview rounds in some colleges. However, most colleges do not require an entrance exam or personal interview for online programmes.
