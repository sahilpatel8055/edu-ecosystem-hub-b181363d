## What Is An Online MBA In Marketing Programme?

[Online MBA degree](https://www.learningroutes.in/courses/online-mba) in Marketing is a **two-year** [**post graduation course**](https://www.learningroutes.in/blog/top-10-online-distance-pg-courses-in-india) offered by various universities in India. You can pursue this course after completing your bachelor’s degree in any stream. Typically, the programme is designed to meet the marketing needs of companies while enhancing their understanding of the business. This course is ideal for candidates who wish to transition into marketing, aim to streamline their career in this domain or are working professionals intending to improve their skills.

## What are The Eligibility Criteria For Online MBA In Marketing Programme?

The online MBA in marketing is available for all students, irrespective of their academic or professional background. However, you must meet certain criteria to enrol in your desired institution.

- ➤

You should have completed a bachelor’s degree from a UGC or AICTE-recognised university.
- ➤

You must have scored more than 50% marks in your previous qualification. This minimum mark requirement can vary for every college.

📌 **Note:** The reserved category students get 5% relaxation. Work experience is not mandatory but may be preferred by a few colleges. Though not compulsory, there are institutions which require entrance exam scores.

## Here’s The Admission Process for Online MBA in Marketing Programme

In some colleges/universities, valid entrance exam scores are also required. In that case, you must first appear in the exam and then fill out the application form. Check the admission procedure steps below.

- ➤

Start by filling out the application form online. Fill in the personal information, qualifications and experience, if any.
- ➤

Submit the documents the university requests, such as identity proof, academic transcripts, etc.
- ➤

Pay the application processing fee.
- ➤

Appear in the personal interview or presentation round (if applicable).
- ➤

Wait for the university to shortlist your profile. Once shortlisted, block your seat by paying the course fee.

## FAQs

### Q1. Is an Online MBA in Marketing worth it?

Undoubtedly, an MBA has ample benefits. If done from a UGC-DEB-approved and recognised university, your online MBA in Marketing is totally worth it. Additionally, if you are pursuing the degree, avail yourself of the benefits, not because everyone else is doing it.

### Q2. How much does an Online MBA in Marketing cost?

The typical MBA marketing online programme fee ranges between INR 50,000 and INR 10,00,000. The government college fee is generally less than that of private institutions.

### Q3. How long does an Online MBA in Marketing take to complete?

Completing an MBA Marketing online programme takes two years or a maximum of four years.

### Q4. What are the admissions requirements for an Online MBA in Marketing?

While every university has different admission requirements, the common eligibility for an online MBA in Marketing is a bachelor’s degree in any stream from a UGC-recognised college/university with 50 – 60% marks.

### Q5. What courses are typically included in an Online MBA in Marketing programme?

This programme typically covers brand management, marketing strategy, consumer behaviour, marketing research, and international marketing, among other subjects.
