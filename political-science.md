## How to Get Admission in an Online MA in Political Science?

Obtaining admission to an Online MA in Political Science is a simple, primarily online procedure. Most institutions prioritise academic qualifications and simple documents over detailed admission procedures.

### Step-by-Step Guide for Online MA Political Science Admission Process

Before choosing a university, make sure to check its recognition and accreditation. Below is a simple step-by-step guide to help you understand the admission process for an Online MA in Political Science:

- **Research Programs:** Visit CollegeSathi to compare universities, course fees, specializations, and accreditation details.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Check the eligibility requirements of your selected university. In most cases, a bachelor’s degree with minimum required marks or CGPA is needed.
- **Fill Out the Online Application Form:** Apply on the university’s official website by filling in your personal and academic details and uploading the required documents.
- **Entrance Test or Direct Admission:** Some universities may conduct an entrance exam, while others offer direct admission based on academic records or work experience.
- **Interview or Personal Assessment (If Required):** Certain universities may hold an online interview to evaluate your suitability for the program.
- **Fee Payment and Enrollment:** Pay the course fees to complete the admission process and confirm your enrollment.

Entrance Exams vs. Direct Admission

Admissions to the Online MA in Political Science at certain universities are based on the performance in entrance tests, such as CUET UG, or online tests conducted by the university itself.

Many universities provide direct admission based on a candidate’s academic record. Selection is usually done using bachelor’s degree marks or the required CGPA, without the need for an entrance test.

### Documents Usually Required for Admission

While the requirements may vary from one university to another, applicants are usually required to submit the following documents:

- Mark sheets and the degree certificate of graduation
- Identity proof: Aadhaar, Passport, or Driving License
- Resume or professional experience certificate
- Passport-size photographs

Note: Check the admission process and documents required, as they may vary for universities.
