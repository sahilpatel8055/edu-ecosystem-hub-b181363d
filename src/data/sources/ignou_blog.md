About IGNOU Online Admissions

Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU) offers admissions to online courses in two sessions. You
can either apply for online courses in the January session or the July session. The IGNOU online courses
admission process involves a few stages. It includes registration, form filling, fee payment, and
submission. Interested candidates are advised to apply only through the official portal of IGNOU.
Continue reading to learn about the step-by-step admission process for IGNOU online courses.

IGNOU Online Courses Eligibility Criteria

Every admission process has a critical aspect, known as eligibility. You cannot apply for a course unless
you are eligible for that. IGNOU online UG, PG, diploma, and certification programmes also require you
to fulfil the minimum eligibility criteria. Here is a quick overview:

    1. Undergraduate Courses: You must have passed a 10+2 certificate from a recognised board.

    2. Postgraduate Courses: A bachelor’s degree in the 10+2+3 format or equivalent from a
       recognised institution is mandatory.

    3. Diploma Courses: You must have passed 10+2 to be eligible for diploma courses. A bachelor’s
       degree is crucial to apply for online PG diploma courses.

    4. Certification Courses: At IGNOU, there are two types of online certification courses. One requires
       completion of 10+2, and the other is a PG certificate that demands a bachelor’s degree.

Step-by-Step Process to Apply for IGNOU Online Courses

To apply for IGNOU online courses, you need to fulfil the minimum eligibility criteria for your desired
course. If you can satisfy the IGNOU eligibility for online courses, you can proceed with the application
process. Here are the comprehensive step-by-step instructions to apply for IGNOU online courses.

Career Opportunities After IGNOU Online MBA

The IGNOU Online MBA is a flexible management degree course for working professionals and graduates
who seek to develop their business, managerial and leadership skills without taking regular classes. The
MBAOL is officially listed on the IGNOU IOP portal as recognized by AICTE as a post-graduation course for
the business and management field and is offered online via IGNOU’s online learning platform. It’s
particularly suitable for students who wish to earn their MBA online and for cheap, and give them the
