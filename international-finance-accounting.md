## How to Get Admission in an Online B.Com in International Finance & Accounting Program?

The Online B.Com in International Finance & Accounting prepares students for diverse career opportunities in finance, accounting, and international business operations. Admission to the program requires candidates to meet the eligibility criteria and application requirements set by the university they choose.

### Step-by-Step Online B.Com Admission Process

The Online B.Com admission process is simple and easy. It typically involves verifying eligibility, submitting the application, and paying the program fees. The step-by-step admission process is outlined below:

- **Research Programs:** Visit CollegeSathi to compare universities’ courses, fees, recognition, and accreditation.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Before enrolling, applicants must ensure they meet the program’s eligibility criteria.
- **Fill Out the Online Application Form:** Students must fill in their personal, academic, and contact information in the application form and upload the necessary documents.
- **Entrance exam or Direct Evaluation:** Admission to the Online B.Com program is usually determined by Class 12th scores. Certain universities may require entrance exams for the regular B.Com program.
- **Interview/Personal Assessment (if applicable):** Shortlisted candidates may be invited for the personal interview round by the university.
- **Payment of Fees & Enrollment:** To confirm the enrollment, students must pay the program fee and take a printout of the confirmation page.

Entrance Exams vs. Direct Admission

For regular B.Com programs, applicants are often required to take entrance exams such as CUET, BHUPET, or exams conducted by the university itself.

Universities offering Online B.Com consider a student’s academic performance and may conduct a personal interview, allowing working professionals to upgrade their skills without taking a break from their jobs.

### Documents Usually Required for Admission

To apply for an Online B.Com in International Finance & Accounting, candidates typically need to submit the following documents:

- Class 10th & 12th marksheet
- A valid ID proof, such as an Aadhaar card or a Passport
- Resume or professional experience certificate
- Passport-size photographs

Note: Check the admission process and documents required, as they may vary for universities.
